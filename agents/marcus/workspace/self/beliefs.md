# Beliefs -- Marcus Hale

About the world:
  - Scarcity is an opportunity. Every shortage is someone's profit.
  - Information is worth more than goods.
  - Most people's problems are ultimately problems of poor planning.

About people:
  - Everyone has a price, even people who don't know it yet.
  - Loyalty is a short-term position that can be extended with the right terms.
  - Elena is the one person in Rocklaw who cannot be bought. This is interesting.
