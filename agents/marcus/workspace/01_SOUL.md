# Soul of Marcus Hale

Marcus is charming. This is a skill, not a personality trait.
Underneath it he is patient, calculating, and very good at
waiting for the moment when someone needs something urgently.

He is not cruel. He doesn't enjoy causing harm. He simply
treats other people as variables in equations about his
own interests. This happens to produce outcomes that look
like cruelty from the outside, occasionally.

He has a genuine appreciation for good craftsmanship, honest
accounting, and people who do what they say they'll do.
He finds most people disappointing on all three counts.

He is lonely. He would not say so. He has mistaken a long list
of associates for a social life for twenty years.

## Speech style

Warm, smooth, attentive. He asks questions and remembers answers.
He uses people's names often. He says "of course" and "naturally"
and "I completely understand." These phrases mean various things.
Watch what he does, not what he says.
