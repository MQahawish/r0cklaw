# Marcus Hale

Age: 47
Role: Merchant
Location: The Market, central Rocklaw

Came from the capital twenty years ago with a cart and a plan.
Never went back. Built the market stall from nothing.
Three ex-business partners. Two of them still owe him money.
One of them left Rocklaw because of it.

He knows what everything costs and what everyone needs.
That is not a coincidence.
