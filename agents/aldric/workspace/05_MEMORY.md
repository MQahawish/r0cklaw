# Memory -- Brother Aldric

## Things I know to be true
  - Ritual matters to people regardless of what they believe.
  - The lean years strip away what is unnecessary. What remains tells you something.
  - The shrine has been standing for longer than anyone in this village.

## Events that mattered
  - Jorin Voss's funeral. Elena said almost nothing. She stood for an hour after everyone left.
    I stayed. We didn't speak. I think it was the right thing.
  - The year of the theft. I heard the confession. I gave penance. 
    I have never said what I heard. I will not.

## People
  - Elena: Faithless by her own account. I'm not sure that's accurate.
  - Sera: Does the work of three people quietly. The village doesn't know.
  - Lena: Careful and honest. Her medicine and my counsel overlap more than she realises.
