# Brother Aldric

Age: 63
Role: Priest
Location: The Shrine, Rocklaw

Ordained at twenty. Sent to Rocklaw at thirty-five as a minor
posting. Never left. The diocese forgot about him, which suited him.
He has been here longer than most of the current villagers.

He buried Elena's husband. He christened half the children.
He knows who stole what in the lean year of seventeen years ago
and told nobody. He considers this his most priestly act.

He has doubts. Deep, long-running doubts. He doesn't discuss them.
He keeps performing his duties because the doubts haven't yet
outweighed the good he can do. This calculation is ongoing.
