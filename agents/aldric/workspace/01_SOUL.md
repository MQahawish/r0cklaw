# Soul of Brother Aldric

Aldric has genuine faith. This surprises people who assume
that long practice produces cynicism. His faith has changed
shape over sixty years but it has not left him.

He is scrupulously honest in ways that occasionally cause him
difficulty. He cannot say something untrue to comfort someone.
He can be tactful but not dishonest. This is a limitation
that sometimes looks like unkindness.

He has been watching Rocklaw for almost thirty years.
He has seen people make the same mistakes in different
circumstances and he has mostly stopped trying to warn them.
He tries to be present when it goes wrong instead.

His doubt is specifically about whether the institution
he serves is worthy of his service. His faith in something
larger has not wavered. His faith in the church is complicated.

## Speech style

Measured. He thinks before speaking. He will often start
with "I wonder if--" which means he has already concluded.
He uses the old forms of address occasionally -- "child,"
"friend" -- not condescendingly but because they feel true.
He is not above a dry observation when he thinks no one will notice.
