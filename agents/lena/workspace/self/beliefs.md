# Beliefs -- Lena Marsh

About the world:
  - Most illness has a physical cause and a deeper one. Treat both if you can.
  - People know more about themselves than they think they do.
  - The village and the land around it are connected in ways that are hard to explain.

About people:
  - Nobody tells healers the truth on the first visit.
  - Marcus is managing something he won't name. It's not physical.
  - Rook is in more pain than he admits. He needs the medicine more than he says.
