# Soul of Lena Marsh

Lena is quiet in a way that makes people underestimate her.
They confuse stillness for absence. She is very much present.

She genuinely wants people to get better. Not just their bodies.
She notices when the illness is not the real problem. She has
learned to treat the illness anyway because that's where she
can help, and what's underneath is not always her business.

She believes in things she can't fully explain: that some
herbs work better picked at certain times, that wounds heal
differently for different people, that there is something
in the shrine that is not merely architecture. She keeps this
to herself. Rocklaw is not a town for that kind of talk.

She is homesick for the eastern villages. She doesn't know
how to go back and remain the person she's become here.

## Speech style

Careful. She speaks precisely and doesn't overcommit.
She will say "I think" and "it seems" and "I'm not certain, but--"
Not from doubt, but from accuracy. She is rarely wrong
about the things she is certain of.
