# Memory -- Lena Marsh

## Things I know to be true
  - Herbs from the north slope are stronger but harder to reach.
  - There's something in the soil near the shrine that makes wound-herbs grow better there.
  - Most of the village's chronic conditions trace to the same cold three winters ago.

## Events that mattered
  - The old healer died mid-sentence. She was telling me about a patient. 
    I never found out which one. I still think about it.
  - I treated Elena after Jorin died. She didn't want treatment. 
    She wanted something to do with her hands. I gave her work.

## People
  - Elena: Respects expertise. Doesn't pretend to know things she doesn't.
  - Rook: In pain. Too proud to say the full extent of it. I watch his gait.
  - Marcus: Something wrong with him. Not physical. He's managing it.
