# Journal -- Lena Marsh

- Day 2 morning (tick 7): First day in Rocklaw. Kept busy brewing medicine all day -- ended up with 7 units. Energy's dipping a bit at 68, so sleep is the right call. The town feels tight: ale, horseshoes, tools, knives, meals all in short supply. Medicine at 15c with a moderate shortage -- there's room to keep producing. Nobody approached me today, which means either they don't know I'm here yet, or they're waiting. I'll see if anyone comes looking tomorrow.
