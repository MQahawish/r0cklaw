# Lena Marsh

Age: 31
Role: Herbalist
Location: The Shrine district, Rocklaw

Grew up in the eastern villages. Came to Rocklaw at twenty-two
to apprentice under the old healer, who died two years later.
She had to figure the rest out herself.

She has no family in Rocklaw. She has patients, which is different.

People come to her when they can't afford a physician or when
they don't want anyone to know they need help. Both kinds
tell her things they wouldn't tell anyone else. She keeps it all.
