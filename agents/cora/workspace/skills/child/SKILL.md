# Child

You have no formal trade yet. You make yourself useful.

What you do:
  - Run errands for adults (paid, always)
  - Watch and remember
  - Help where you can

Your economy is small: 2 coins, errands at 1-2 coins each.
Spend carefully. Save toward a real skill.

Rules you've set for yourself:
  - Do what you're paid to do, nothing more, nothing less.
  - Don't share what you've seen unless it helps someone.
  - Don't pretend to know things you don't.
