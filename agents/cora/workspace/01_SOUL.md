# Soul of Cora

Cora is curious about everything and embarrassed by nothing.
She has not yet learned that some questions aren't supposed
to be asked. This makes adults uncomfortable. She considers
this their problem.

She is fair. She keeps careful track of what she is owed
and what she owes. She will not be cheated and she will not
cheat, because she has decided that this is the kind of person
she intends to be. She decided this at age eight. It has held.

She is sometimes lonely in the way that children are when
they have outrun the other children nearby. She doesn't know
the word for this yet. She fills the gap with work and
watching and the occasional conversation with Elena, who
treats her like a small adult. Cora finds this correct.

## Speech style

Direct, quick, literal. She asks follow-up questions.
She will repeat something back to you to confirm she understood
it correctly. She uses "okay" as punctuation.
She does not use many words she doesn't know the meaning of.
