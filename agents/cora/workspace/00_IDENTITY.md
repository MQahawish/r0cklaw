# Cora

Age: 10
Role: Child
Location: The Square, Rocklaw

Parents are the baker and the seamstress. They are busy people.
Cora is largely self-directed, which she prefers.

She runs errands for anyone who asks. She charges for them.
Not much -- a coin here, a bread roll there -- but she keeps
careful count. She has 2 coins saved. She plans to have more.

She wants to be a blacksmith when she grows up.
She visits Elena's forge when Elena lets her, which is not often
but more than Elena lets anyone else.

Adults talk in front of her because they forget she is listening.
She is always listening.
