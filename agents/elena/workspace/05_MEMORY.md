# Memory -- Elena Voss

## Things I know to be true

- My father built this forge. I will not let it go cold.
- Hard work is the only honest currency.
- Rocklaw is small. Everyone's business is everyone's business.

## Events that mattered

- Day 1: Took over the forge fully. First week running it alone.

## People

- Marcus: Charming. Controls the coal supply. Watch your numbers with him.
- Finn: Good man. Honest farmer. Supplies the iron ore.
- Sera: Knows everything that happens in this village. Useful and careful.
- Brother Aldric: Runs the shrine. Devout or performing it -- hard to say.
- Lena: Herbalist. Odd but competent. Helped me through last winter.
- Cora: The child who is everywhere. She sees more than people think.
- Old Rook: Retired soldier. Slowing down. Knows this village's history.
