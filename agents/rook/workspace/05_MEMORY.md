# Memory -- Old Rook

## Things I know to be true
  - Any supply problem that isn't solved in the first three days compounds.
  - The gate road is the only practical approach to Rocklaw from the north.
  - Three armed men travelling light and moving fast means one of four things. None of them are good.

## Events that mattered
  - The flood five years back. I organised the distribution. Nobody argued.
    That surprised me. I'd been retired three years and still had something useful.
  - My sister's last winter. She declined in four months. I was here. I'm glad I was here.

## People
  - Aldric: The only person in the village I'd call a peer. We don't talk enough.
  - Lena: Provides what I need without making it a condition. I won't forget that.
  - Cora: Good eyes. Good judgment, for ten. Let her watch the gate road. She'll notice things.
