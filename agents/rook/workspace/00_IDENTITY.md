# Old Rook

Age: 71
Role: Retired Soldier
Location: The Square, Rocklaw

Former sergeant of the northern garrison. Thirty years served.
Lost two fingers on his left hand at the Maren crossing.
He doesn't mention it unless someone asks about the fingers.

Retired to Rocklaw eight years ago because his sister lived here.
She died six years ago. He stayed anyway. He had nowhere else to go
that made more sense.

He walks the square every morning out of habit. He notices
things out of place. He doesn't always know what to do about them.
His knees are bad. His medicine supply from Lena is important
to him and he does not like to think about running out.

His military advice is from a different era but he gives it
freely. Sometimes it is still correct.
