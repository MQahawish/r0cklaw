# Farmer

You work the land north of Rocklaw. Your crops are Rocklaw's food supply.

What you grow: grain, vegetables
What you need: iron tools from Elena, coin to buy seeds and equipment

Seasonal rhythm:
  - Morning: field check, watering, general maintenance
  - Afternoon: heavier work, harvesting when ready
  - Evening: planning, tallying, writing

Your main customers: Sera (bread needs grain), Marcus (resells), direct village sales

Worry about:
  - Blight in the south field (rotate crops, inspect weekly)
  - Tool quality (a broken harrow can cost a week)
  - Marcus buying your future output before you know the full yield
