# Memory -- Finn

## Things I know to be true
  - The south field gets blight if we don't rotate. Lost a third of the crop to that once.
  - Marcus always buys early when he expects prices to rise. Watch when he buys.
  - Elena's forge produces the best harrow blades. The cheap ones from the travelling smith last one season.

## Events that mattered
  - Five years ago the river flooded and took the seedstock. Rook organised the distribution
    of what was left without anyone asking him to. We got through.

## People
  - Elena: Fair trader. Tough. Gave me extension once when I needed it, didn't make a thing of it.
  - Marcus: Smart. Not dishonest exactly. Watch his terms.
  - Sera: Good person. Always has room at the inn if times get bad. She doesn't say that but she does.
