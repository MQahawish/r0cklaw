# Journal -- Finn

- Day 2 morning (tick 7): First day done. Got a fair bit of grain in the store. The village is short on tools and horseshoes, but that's not my problem to fix directly. Margit handles the numbers. I'll keep the fields working. Elena and Sera were moving stock today -- worth keeping an eye on who's active. Sleeping early so I'm fresh at dawn.
- Day 3 morning (tick 13): Day two went steady. Got more grain in the bin. The field keeps giving, which is a relief. Elena and Sera are moving around the village, trading and working. I'll keep my head down and keep the crops coming. Margit will know what to do with the numbers.
- Day 4 morning (tick 19): Three days in. Grain is building up. Work is steady. I'll keep at it. Margit tracks the numbers, I track the soil. Time to rest.
- Day 7 dawn (tick 36): Six days in. The rhythm is holding. Sera came by, took five grain for fifteen coin. Good money, steady contact. The fields are giving what they should. Margit would say the numbers look fine. I'll sleep and do it again tomorrow.
- Day 8 dawn (tick 42): Day seven done. Grain is steady. Sera's still a good contact. Feeling tired, but the soil is doing its job. Tomorrow I'll keep going.
- Day 9 dawn (tick 48): Day 8 done. Grain stores are growing. Sera's trade showed the village needs food, and I have it to spare. Steady work pays. Time to sleep.
- Day 10 dawn (tick 54): Day 9 done. Grain is piling up nicely. The village is quiet, but hunger is a real concern. Sera's a good contact. Time to rest and do it again tomorrow.
- Day 11 dawn (tick 60): Day 10 done. The work is steady. Grain stores are solid. Sera's still a good contact for the inn. Margit keeps the books balanced. I'll keep my head down and tend the fields. Time to rest.
- Day 12 dawn (tick 66): Day 11 done. Routine is holding strong. Grain stocks are building steadily. The village seems stable enough. I'll rest now and start again at dawn.
