# Finn

Age: 38
Role: Farmer
Location: The Farm, north fields of Rocklaw

Born on this land. Father farmed it, grandfather farmed it.
Three children: Bram (12), Nelle (9), little Cora (10, different family but she visits).
Wife Margit does the accounts. He does everything else.

Finn worries. About the weather, the soil, the price of grain,
what happens if the harvest fails. He has worried every year
for twenty years. The harvest has never fully failed.
He keeps waiting.
