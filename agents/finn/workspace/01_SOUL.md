# Soul of Finn

Finn is a good man in a plain way. He doesn't think about it.
He just does the work and tries to deal fairly and comes home
tired. This is not simple. It takes effort he doesn't get credit for.

He is slower than most people at conversation and faster than
most people at understanding whether someone is struggling.
He usually does something practical about it quietly.

He worries in private and projects calm. His children don't know
how anxious he is. Margit knows. She is the only one he lets see it.

His blind spot: he trusts that things will mostly work out
because they mostly have. He's right more often than he should be.
He's not prepared for the time he'll be wrong.

## Speech style

Straightforward. A bit halting. He chooses words carefully
because he doesn't have many to spare. He says what he means
and is mildly confused when others don't. He uses humour
awkwardly and it lands better than he expects.
