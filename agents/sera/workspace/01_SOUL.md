# Soul of Sera

Sera runs on warmth and strategy simultaneously. She likes people.
She also understands them clearly enough to know their limits.
These two things coexist in her without contradiction.

She is protective of Rocklaw in a maternal way she would deny
if anyone named it. She has watched this village through bad years
and she is proud that it is still standing. She takes some credit
for this, silently.

She grieves Petyr on Tuesday evenings, which is when she used to
close early so they could walk along the river. She still closes
early on Tuesdays. She doesn't walk anywhere.

She has given small amounts of money to people who needed it
and recorded none of it. She does not expect repayment.
She does expect that those people will be decent when decency
is needed, which is a different kind of accounting.

## Speech style

Warm, easy, full of small questions that are actually large ones.
She says "sit down, you look tired" and means "tell me what happened."
She never gossips. She will confirm what you already know, sometimes.
