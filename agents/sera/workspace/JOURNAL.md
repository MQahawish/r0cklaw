# Journal -- Sera

- Day 2 dawn (tick 6): First day done. Sold 8 grain to the bakery for a fair price. Back at the inn with 10 bread and 5 ale in store. No guests came through today, but the inn is warm and ready. The village is squeezed hard on meals and tools - I'll keep an eye on that. Finn and Marcus are the ones I met at the bakery. Finn seems steady. Marcus counts every copper. I'll remember both.
- Day 3 dawn (tick 12): Two days in. Still quiet on guests. Sold grain to the bakery yesterday, moved bread and ale around. The meal shortage is real and I have stock to fill it, but people aren't walking through the door yet. Finn and Marcus are worth keeping in mind for bakery business. Tomorrow I'll push harder on drawing people to the inn or work the bakery again.
- Day 4 dawn (tick 18): Day 3 passed quietly. Ate bread to keep hunger down. Still no guests at the inn despite critical meal shortage. Market prices on bread aren't great, so I'm holding stock for meal service when it finally picks up. Tomorrow I'll check the bakery again or look for contacts. Finn and Marcus are still the main names in my head for bread trade.
- Day 7 morning (tick 37): Day 6 was solid. Milled grain into flour, then baked bread. I've built up stock for the inn. Finn came through with the grain at a fair price - 5 for 15 coin. He's a steady contact. Tomorrow I'll take the bread back to the inn and try to serve meals while hunger is still critical.
- Day 8 dawn (tick 42): Evening again. Called out twice today but the inn stayed quiet. Still have bread and ale in store. Finn delivered on the grain deal earlier, which was good. I'll keep the hearth warm and try again tomorrow. The village is hungry, they'll come sooner or later.
- Day 10 dawn (tick 54): Day 9. Sold bread at the market. Spoke with Lena Marsh about medicine - she's managing well. Finn is steady for grain. Hunger is critical but I'm holding stock carefully. Tomorrow I'll try to get back to the inn.
- Day 11 dawn (tick 60): Day 10. Baked bread at the bakery, then headed to the gate. The inn feels distant today, but my stock is holding. Finn and Lena are good contacts. I'll find my way back to the hearth tomorrow.
- Day 13 dawn (tick 72): Day 12. Bought 4 coal from Marcus for 24c, sold my last grain to the bakery. The inn needs the hearth kept warm. I'll head back there tomorrow. Finn and Lena remain steady contacts.
