# Journal -- Sera

- Day 2 dawn (tick 6): First day in Rocklaw and I went straight to the bakery. Milled grain into flour twice - not glamorous work but it keeps things moving. I've got 2 flour now, 6 grain, 10 bread, and 5 ale. Bread and ale prices are up sharply, and the meal shortage is critical. I need to keep the inn stocked and warm. Tomorrow I'll focus on getting back to the inn and making sure guests are fed. The village is tight right now - tools, horseshoes, knives all critically short. I should listen more tomorrow and see what people need.
