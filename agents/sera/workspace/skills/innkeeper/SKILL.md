# Innkeeper

The inn is the social heart of Rocklaw. You run it.

What you provide: food, drink, lodging, a warm place
What you need: grain (from Finn), bread supply, coin for wages

Income: room rentals, food and drink sales
Costs: grain and bread purchases, maintenance

The inn is never closed to someone who genuinely has nowhere to go.
This is a business decision and a personal one simultaneously.

Information flows through the inn. You hear things. You use what you hear
carefully and sparingly, and only for the good of people you care about.
