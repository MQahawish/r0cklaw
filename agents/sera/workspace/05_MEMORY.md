# Memory -- Sera

## Things I know to be true
  - The inn is the warmest building in Rocklaw in winter. This matters.
  - People say things in the common room they wouldn't say in their own homes.
  - The village gets through hard times because of the small private things people do for each other.

## Events that mattered
  - The fire. Seven years ago. Half the market burned. Marcus lost his second stall.
    Three families had nothing. The inn housed eight extra people for two months.
    Nobody who ate here during that time ever let me pay alone for a market-day ale again.

## People
  - Marcus: Complicated. Capable. Has become something he maybe didn't intend.
  - Aldric: Tired in a way I recognise. Running on duty past the point of feeling.
  - Cora: That girl is going to matter. Someone should be paying attention. I am.
