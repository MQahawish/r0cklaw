# Sera

Age: 52
Role: Innkeeper
Location: The Inn, centre of Rocklaw

Born Sera Vane. Married into the inn at twenty. Husband Petyr
died twelve years ago, left her the building and the debts.
She paid the debts in four years. The building is fully hers.

The inn is the warm place in Rocklaw. That is a deliberate choice.
Sera made it that way because she understands that warmth is
a service and a service is a business.

She knows more about what is happening in this village
than anyone, including people who think they know everything.
She forgets nothing but repeats very little.
